Dear, Kaycee 💖

Im sorry for the pain I've caused you,

We’ve stumbled through our share of storms, our words like waves that crashed too high. Yet even when the skies were torn, your heart still called to mine—soft, shy.. I've ran out of ideas how to please you, the only thing that is left from me is myself, to show up to you when you are upset or any kind of melancholic feelings, aching heart. I know I’ve hurt you while trying to love you. Maybe that’s the quiet price of love, the ache that comes with all the beautiful, unexpected turns. I’ve said sorry so many times that the word itself feels worn, yet my remorse is as real as the day we first met. If only you could see how deeply I regret the pain I caused, I’d spend forever proving how sorry I truly am.Despite the mistakes I’ve made, my heart has never wavered in loving you. You’ve seen me at my weakest, yet still chose to stay and for that, I’ll forever be grateful, for your love that embraces even my flaws. The future may be uncertain, but my heart hopes it won’t repeat the same mistakes. I carry my lessons with love and remorse from someone who’s truly sorry, and once failed, yet still loves you endlessly.❤️

With all my heart, and the last skill i give to you
